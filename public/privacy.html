Privacy

This site is a simple information page. We keep what it collects to the minimum.

What we collect

The site has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, company and message you enter, so we can reply to you.

Contact form

Contact-form messages are delivered to us by Formspree, which processes the message only to send it to our inbox.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

We use your message and address only to reply to you, and we don't share them or add you to mailing lists.
